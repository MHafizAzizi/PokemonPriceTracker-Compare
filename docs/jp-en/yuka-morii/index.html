JP vs EN Prices

Yuka Morii collection, every Japanese print: what the Japanese copy sells for in Japan (Mercari) against the English card on TCGplayer. Pick a card to see its price trend.

Japan price = median of filtered raw Mercari sales (lots, slabs, overseas copies dropped). Difference = EN price − Japan price; a verdict needs the JP copy under 0.8× (cheaper) or over 1.25× (dearer) the EN price. Mercari's minimum is ¥300, so bulk commons always look dearer in Japan. Mercari can't split holo / mirror finishes. Old cards that print no number are matched by name and set, which can mix in other prints. Monthly lines: Mercari months with fewer than 3 sales are blank; TCGplayer lines from api.cardprice.com (JP prints only from 2026-07). JPY/USD = monthly mean of daily ECB rates. Updated .
